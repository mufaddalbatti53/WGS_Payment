Core Capabilities
Everything your finance team needs
💰
Smart Payment Tracking
Record credits, debits, and calculate outstanding balances instantly. Never lose track of a client's financial standing.
📊
Aging Analysis
Automatically categorizes unpaid invoices into 0-30, 30-60, 60-90, and up to 180+ days buckets for clear financial visibility.
🔔
Automated Reminders
Send one-click WhatsApp and Email payment reminders. Set auto-snooze logic to intelligently delay reminders when needed.
📄
Tally & Excel Export
Export perfectly formatted Excel sheets instantly ready to be mapped and imported directly into Tally software.
☁️
Google Drive Backup
Secure, WhatsApp-style silent background database backups to your connected Google Drive account.
👥
Activity Logging
Every action (create, edit, delete, backup) is permanently logged to an Activity stream, holding the team accountable.
